# SignBridge+ Pitch

## 20-second pitch

> "What if your phone could learn a new gesture in 30 seconds? SignBridge+ is an on-device few-shot gesture-learning system that learns personalized gestures from a few examples, recognizes them in real time, and speaks them without the cloud. We demonstrate it through Indian Sign Language communication."

## 60-second pitch

> "Most gesture recognition systems begin with a fixed vocabulary: the model knows certain gestures because they were predefined or trained beforehand. We flip that interaction. SignBridge+ lets the user teach the phone a new gesture from a few examples. A lightweight on-device AI pipeline converts the gesture's temporal movement into a representation, creates a personalized prototype, and recognizes later occurrences locally. The judge can teach the phone a gesture live, repeat it, and hear the phone speak the result. Then we remove connectivity and demonstrate that the core path still works. Indian Sign Language is our first high-impact application, while the underlying engine is a general few-shot gesture-learning mechanism."

## Core positioning

Do not lead with:
> "Another AI sign-language translator."

Lead with:
> "A phone that learns your gesture."

## Open Innovation explanation

> "Our innovation is a reusable on-device few-shot gesture-learning engine. ISL is the first high-impact application, rather than the entire technical scope."

## Closing

> "We don't want users to learn the machine. We want the machine to learn the user."
