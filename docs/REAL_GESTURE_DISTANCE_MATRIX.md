## 3. Raw vs. Normalized Landmark Motion Loss Proof

Mathematical evaluation of pure global arm translation (moving a rigid hand from $X=0.3$ to $X=0.7$ across screen) vs. pure finger articulation (stationary wrist flexing fingers):

```
=== RAW VS NORMALIZED MOTION ANALYSIS ===
Raw Wrist X Displacement:        0.4000 (Screen Space)
Normalized Wrist X Displacement: 0.0000 (Wrist-relative)
Normalized Mean Velocity:        0.000000 (All motion cancelled by wrist origin translation)
Normalized Articulation Velocity: 0.041700 (Preserved when fingers flex relative to wrist)
```

### Mathematical Consequence:
- When a gesture relies on **arm movement** or **global spatial translation** without finger shape changes, translation normalization to the wrist completely zeroes out the motion signal ($v = 0.0000$).
- When a gesture relies on **finger flexion/extension**, normalized velocity is fully preserved ($v = 0.0417$).
